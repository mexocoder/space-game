DESIGN EVALUATION v0.1 Prototype

What Works Great (Отличные идеи):
  • High Satisfaction: Puncturing the central heart with slow-mo + particle explosion feels immensely rewarding.
  • Clear Contrast: Smooth rolling circle vs rotating sharp polygons creates immediate readable gameplay.
  • Dynamic Risk/Reward: Aiming for the center heart risks hitting outer sharp edges.
What Needs Tuning (Что стоит доработать):
  • Angle Vulnerability: Outer sharp edges need clear visual warning indicators right before they rotate/strike.
  • Dash Aiming: Mouse-direction dash feels crisp, but mobile touch-swipe requires auto-aim assist toward nearest heart.
  • Heart Armor: Boss hearts need rotating shield plates so the player must timing-click through gaps.
